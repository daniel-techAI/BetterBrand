# R/CREATION: Drop 001 Launch Strategy

## Position

R/CREATION is not another graphic-clothing account. It is the physical layer of a connected world:

- **R/CREATION:** wearable emotional artifacts.
- **Hades Theory:** the ideas, writing, symbols, and chapters beneath the garments.
- **Footwear:** the founder's hand-built proof that the world is made, not only posted.
- **Slovakia:** the real place the brand begins, not a detail to hide.

The first launch should make one promise: **Emotions are architecture. Rebuild yourself.**

## What Rising Independent Brands Prove

### Corteiz: a symbol people can belong to

Corteiz built around one recognizable symbol, local identity, limited access, social-first marketing, and physical activations. The repeatable lesson is not artificial scarcity. It is making entry into the brand feel like entry into a shared point of view.

**R/CREATION application:** keep the split face, halo, serpent, loop, and thorn-star inside one emotional system. Use the private-access list for sample votes and early entry, not fake countdowns.

### Represent: the founder is the strongest media channel

George Heaton describes the founder as the brand's biggest influencer. People follow the decisions, standards, failures, and progress because those make the product believable.

**R/CREATION application:** show Daniel drawing, rejecting samples, making footwear, testing fits, and explaining one Hades Theory idea at a time. The founder should appear regularly, even when the content is filmed simply.

### Rare Humans: cadence beats random posting

The Vienna label uses a consistent drop rhythm, begins content two to three weeks before release, works across Instagram and TikTok, and gives its local community something to participate in.

**R/CREATION application:** build every release as a 21-day chapter with a reveal sequence. Start locally and visually. Do not wait for a large following before creating participation.

### Nude Project: clothes are an entry into culture

Nude Project treats its social presence as a public journal and uses founders, conversations, and real gatherings to make the label feel larger than garments.

**R/CREATION application:** use Hades Theory, footwear craft, music, sketches, and conversations about reconstruction as the culture. Clothes become the artifact a person can own.

## Drop 001 Capsule

Launch with four purchasable products, not a crowded catalog:

1. **Face What You Hide Hoodie** - hero product.
2. **Rebuild Yourself Crop Hoodie** - second hero and women's chapter.
3. **Embrace The Chaos Crop Tee** - lower entry price.
4. **Architecture Socks** - low-risk add-on.

Keep the Unavailable and Prison caps as the next small release unless the production samples are excellent. AirPod cases, stickers, and phone cases can follow only after the visual system is proven.

## Product Gate

No item goes live until it passes all five checks:

1. The message is readable at normal viewing distance.
2. The artwork placement works on a real body, not only a mockup.
3. The blank weight, fit, print, and wash result match the price.
4. The margin remains healthy after production, payment fees, VAT, and expected reprints.
5. One ten-second video can communicate why the piece exists.

Order one men's hoodie sample and one women's hoodie or tee sample first. Photograph the real samples before spending on ads.

## 21-Day Launch Sequence

### Days 21-15: Build the world

- Post the split-face symbol without selling.
- Publish one Hades Theory statement as a short video.
- Show one footwear-making detail beside one apparel detail.
- Open the First 100 list and ask one real sample question.

### Days 14-8: Show the work

- Reveal the front, then the back, then the fit.
- Show the Printify sample arriving and inspect it honestly.
- Post one rejected detail and the correction.
- Let the audience vote on black versus charcoal or one placement decision.

### Days 7-2: Make the drop concrete

- Announce the date, products, prices, sizing, production model, and delivery estimate.
- Publish one founder video explaining why the collection exists.
- Give the private list first access.
- Ask five small creators whose style genuinely fits the world to wear or review a sample. Do not buy generic shoutouts.

### Launch day and after

- Open the store at one clear time.
- Pin one product video, one founder/story video, and one sizing/ordering post.
- Repost customers and answer every sizing question quickly.
- Publish production updates until every order is delivered.
- Turn returns, fit questions, and comments into the next product revision.

## Content Engine

Use five repeatable series:

1. **Theory in 15 seconds:** one idea, one sentence, one symbol.
2. **Build the artifact:** sketch, placement, print file, sample, revision.
3. **Made by the founder:** footwear craft and apparel decisions in the same frame.
4. **Two sides / same war:** women's and men's chapters shown together.
5. **The First 100 decides:** real votes with the result shown later.

Film vertically for TikTok and Reels first. Recut the strongest process videos for YouTube Shorts. Long-form YouTube can wait until there is enough real process for a useful episode.

## Channel Order

1. **Shopify with a custom domain:** primary brand home, checkout, customer data, Printify connection, and Instagram catalog.
2. **Instagram:** visual world, founder journal, product proof, and customer identity.
3. **TikTok:** discovery through process, honest revisions, and Hades Theory hooks.
4. **Etsy later:** test stickers, socks, or art-led accessories if they fit Etsy's current production-partner rules.
5. **Amazon later:** only after a hero product, reliable fulfillment, and repeatable demand are proven.

Splitting a tiny launch across too many marketplaces creates duplicate work and weakens the brand signal. Own the customer relationship first.

## EUR 200-250 Test Budget

- **EUR 90-120:** two production samples, including delivery.
- **EUR 25-40:** custom domain and initial store costs.
- **EUR 25-40:** simple shoot needs, local travel, or one location fee.
- **EUR 30-50:** creator seeding or sample delivery.
- **EUR 20-30:** ads only after an organic post proves it can hold attention.
- **EUR 20:** error and reprint buffer.

Do not spend the ad budget until the samples, product page, sizing, shipping estimate, and checkout are all real.

## First Metrics

For the first drop, measure proof rather than vanity:

- 100 private-access emails.
- 20 meaningful replies, votes, or sizing questions.
- 10 sample-page visits from each strong content post.
- 3% or better product-page add-to-bag rate once traffic is relevant.
- First 10 paid orders delivered without avoidable support problems.
- At least 3 customer photos or videos that can support Drop 002.

## Legal and Trust Gate

- Search `R/CREATION`, `Hades Theory`, collection names, and symbols in EUIPO eSearch plus and TMview before labels or packaging are ordered.
- The phrase **Emotionally Unavailable** is already used by an established apparel label. The site therefore calls the cap **Unavailable Edition**. Obtain professional clearance before selling apparel or headwear with the full phrase.
- Confirm commercial rights for every artwork, font, mockup, photograph, and generated image.
- Publish the real Slovak business identity, address, contact, VAT status, shipping window, return terms, privacy policy, terms, and cookie consent before accepting orders.
- Configure marketing email consent and keep proof of opt-in.
- Do not claim sustainable, handmade, limited, or Slovak-made unless the production facts support the exact wording.
